# Immersive lyrics

The old reader mixed serif paragraphs, rectangular focus blocks and a dense fixed toolbar. The redesign uses one real sentence per visual unit, soft emphasis and a stable control layer. It follows purposeful, cancellable motion and restrained control materials from [Apple motion guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/motion) and [materials guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials). No Apple artwork, trademarks or proprietary font files are copied.

Two real-browser alternatives were compared: indigo/purple with stronger system text, and graphite/slate with softer text. The final treatment combines indigo atmosphere with system semibold text. Private QA evidence lives under .local/design-v1.3: candidate screenshots, ten selected states, five consecutive transition frames, a recording and a separate single-browser WebKit performance trace. Final formal screenshots are generated by the HTTPS UI tests.

Font stack: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Segoe UI, PingFang SC, Microsoft YaHei, sans-serif. No font download. English units are 23–28px on phones and up to 38px on desktops, weight 600, line height about 1.4; focus adds luminance, a tiny optical emphasis and 1.009 scale without changing line wrapping. Previous/next units remain readable and unblurred. The source's alternatives and slots stay intact. Tablet/desktop use a narrow sidebar; phone landscape uses horizontal chapter navigation with lyrics left and controls at the lower right.

Atmosphere uses two static radial-gradient fields, a slow transform only while visible/playing, and a 2.2-second chapter crossfade. Initial rendering has no full-screen fade. Only the control and settings layers use substantial backdrop blur; a painted scroll edge prevents text bleeding through navigation. Reduced motion disables transitions; unavailable backdrop filters get opaque readable surfaces. Sustained slow animation frames automatically stop ambient drift and simplify the control material.

The focus coordinator uses actual audio cue IDs, the visible navigation/dock bounds and a 380ms bounded easing curve. It does not infer word timing. Large sentences anchor below navigation and remain naturally scrollable. Wheel/touch/pointer/keyboard browsing cancels motion and holds position until explicit return. Repeating the same sentence, including its silence, does not trigger another transition. Click selection stays separate from follow highlighting; native file loops and the persistent audio element remain unchanged.

The native range control retains its accessible keyboard/touch semantics with a thin custom track and a 44px hit area. Dragging previews time locally; release commits once, cancellation restores the real position, and audio callbacks cannot overwrite an active drag. The visible progress follows the real clock, with requestAnimationFrame stopped while hidden or paused. Background/foreground and chapter changes re-anchor only when manual browsing is not active.

No materials, pronunciation rules, voice settings or audio bytes change in this release. All content and playback gates remain, including real native loops and formal HTTPS byte checks. Desktop/mobile emulation, ASR and frame captures do not certify physical iPhone lock-screen, interruptions or hardware frame rates.
